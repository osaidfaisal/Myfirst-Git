FAQ

Do I need to pay for Claude?

No. Everything in this cheat sheet works on Claude's free plan. You only need an email or Google account to sign up at claude.ai or in the Claude app.

I'm not a tech person. Is this too advanced?

It's written for complete beginners. If you can send a WhatsApp message, you can use Claude, and the cheat sheet shows you exactly what to type.

Isn't using AI cheating?

It depends on how you use it, and every class has its own rules. The cheat sheet teaches you to use Claude to explain, quiz, plan and give feedback while you do the real work yourself. It also includes a section on checking each class's AI policy.

Is this official UC Davis or Anthropic material?

No. It's an independent student resource and isn't affiliated with UC Davis or Anthropic (the company that makes Claude).

What will you do with my email?

We'll send you the cheat sheet and occasional useful tips for students. We never sell your email, and you can unsubscribe any time.
